Browser / puzzle game02

Arrange. Compare. Score.

Ett litet spel

A number-placement puzzle on a 4 × 4 board. Drag randomly drawn tiles into place and try to form increasing sequences across rows and columns. Total points and the number of games are stored locally in the browser.

What to explore: drag-and-drop interaction, sequence scoring, and local storage.

JavaScriptHTML / CSSlocalStorage

What to explore: object-oriented battle rules, party state, and exploration-to-battle transitions.

JavaSwingOOP
